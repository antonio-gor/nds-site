05 / About NDS

Three decades of making the difficult feel inevitable.

Noonan Door and Sash is a licensed custom woodworking shop in San Marcos, serving projects across San Diego County.

Our experience is in work that cannot simply be ordered: unusual openings, exact architectural matches, complex fabrication, and cabinetry that feels at home in the room. Clients work directly with the people responsible for making their project.
